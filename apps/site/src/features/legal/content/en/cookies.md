# Cookie policy

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Browser storage (local storage and session storage) works in a similar way. This page describes which of these svanda.fun uses and why.

The website uses no third-party cookies or storage, and no advertising or tracking cookies.

## Necessary

Without these items the website does not work the way you set it up. We store them under Section 89(3) of the Czech Electronic Communications Act (No. 127/2005 Coll.), which implements Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive and requires no consent for them. The data stays in your browser and is not sent to us.

- **cc_cookie** (cookie): remembers your choices in the cookie settings so we do not ask on every visit. Expires after 6 months.
- **svanda-theme** (local storage): the light or dark appearance you chose. Kept until you clear it in your browser.
- **svanda-lang** (local storage): the language you chose in the language switch. Kept until you clear it in your browser.
- **svanda-jukebox-config** (local storage): the colours you set in the jukebox configurator. They are sent to us only if you attach them to the contact form yourself. Kept until you clear it in your browser.
- **svanda-intro-seen** (session storage): the intro animation has already played, so it does not play again. Kept until you close the tab.

## Analytics

We measure visits with our own Umami instance (stats.svanda.fun), which uses no cookies and stores nothing on your device. The measurement is based on our legitimate interest and you can switch it off at any time in the cookie settings; the cc_cookie cookie remembers your choice. More in the [Privacy policy](/en/privacy).

## Marketing

We do not use marketing cookies at the moment. If we add any in the future, they will load only with your prior consent, they will be off by default, and we will update this page beforehand.

## Changing your choices

You can change your choices at any time through the Cookie settings link in the website footer. You can also delete or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings; the website may then forget, for example, the appearance you chose.

The Instagram and YouTube links lead to websites run by their operators, where their own cookie policies apply.
