# Privacy policy

This policy explains which personal data we process when you visit svanda.fun or write to us through the contact form, why we do it and what rights you have. We follow Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the Czech Personal Data Processing Act (No. 110/2019 Coll.).

For anything about personal data, contact us at [{{privacyEmail}}](mailto:{{privacyEmail}}). We have not appointed a data protection officer, as the law does not require us to.

## Contact form

When you send us an enquiry, we process the data you enter in the form: your name, the name of your business or venue, your e-mail address, your message (optional) and, if you attach it, a jukebox colour configuration. We also record the language of the website you sent the form from.

- **Purpose:** to answer your enquiry and, where relevant, prepare an offer or a contract. We also send a confirmation with a copy of your enquiry to your e-mail address.
- **Legal basis:** taking steps at your request prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). For general questions, our legitimate interest in answering people who contact us (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Retention:** while we handle your enquiry and then for no longer than 2 years after our last communication. If we enter into a contract, we keep the data for the term of the contract and then for as long as accounting and tax laws require.

Providing the data is voluntary, but without your name and e-mail address we cannot reply.

## Abuse protection

The form is protected by ALTCHA, which runs on our own server: your browser solves a short computing task, with no cookies and no third-party services. To limit spam and attacks, the server briefly keeps your IP address in memory to cap the number of submissions. We do not write your IP address into form records and forget it once the limit expires.

- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in securing the website and preventing abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Running the website

On every visit, the web server necessarily processes technical data such as your IP address, the time of the request and your browser type, so that it can deliver the page and protect it against attacks. Where this data is written to technical server logs, we keep it for no longer than 30 days.

- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in running the website securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The fonts, images and scripts of the website are served from our own server, so no third party is contacted when you visit.

## Visitor analytics

We measure visits with Umami, which we run ourselves on our own server (stats.svanda.fun). Umami uses no cookies, stores nothing on your device and builds no visitor profiles. It records the pages viewed, where you came from, your device and browser type, your country and page performance. It does not store your IP address; it uses it only to determine your country and to create an anonymous visit identifier that changes regularly.

- **Purpose:** to learn which parts of the website people use and to improve it.
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in measuring and improving the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Retention:** aggregated statistics for no longer than 2 years.
- **Objection:** you can switch the measurement off at any time in the cookie settings in the website footer. Details are in the [Cookie policy](/en/cookies).

## Processors and recipients

We do not sell your personal data or share it for marketing. Only the following processors handle it, under a data processing agreement:

- **Hetzner Online GmbH**, Germany: hosting of the server that runs the website, the form and the analytics. The data stays in data centres in the EU.
- **Google Ireland Limited**, Ireland (Google Workspace): sending and storing e-mail, including messages from the form.

We may also disclose data to public authorities where the law requires it.

## Transfers outside the EU

Google may also process data outside the European Economic Area, in particular in the USA. Such transfers are covered by the European Commission adequacy decision for the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, under which Google is certified, and by standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. We make no other transfers outside the EU.

## Your rights

You have the right:

- to access your personal data (Art. 15 GDPR),
- to have inaccurate data rectified (Art. 16 GDPR),
- to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
- to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
- to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
- to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR),
- to withdraw consent where processing is based on it, at any time and without affecting earlier processing.

To exercise your rights, e-mail us at [{{privacyEmail}}](mailto:{{privacyEmail}}). We reply without undue delay and within one month at the latest.

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.

## Complaints

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In the Czech Republic this is the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Prague 7, [www.uoou.gov.cz](https://www.uoou.gov.cz). You can also complain to the supervisory authority of the EU country where you live or work.
